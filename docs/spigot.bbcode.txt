[CENTER][SIZE=6][B]JustLogs[/B][/SIZE]
Block and container logging with an inspector, lookup and rollback. Know who broke, placed, burned, blew up or took what, and put it back.[/CENTER]

[SIZE=5][B]Features[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Logs blocks placed and broken by players, water and lava buckets, fire (ignite, burn), explosions, tree growth and block changes by mobs (enderman, farmland trampling).
[*]Knows who is behind it: fire and TNT are attributed to the player who lit them, otherwise to an honest source such as [ICODE]#fire[/ICODE], [ICODE]#tnt[/ICODE] or [ICODE]#creeper[/ICODE].
[*]Logs items put into and taken out of chests, barrels, shulker boxes, hoppers, furnaces and other containers, including named and enchanted items.
[*]Free-form search: [ICODE]u:[/ICODE] player, [ICODE]t:[/ICODE] time ([ICODE]2d[/ICODE], [ICODE]2d-1d[/ICODE]), [ICODE]r:[/ICODE] radius, [ICODE]a:[/ICODE] action or group, [ICODE]b:[/ICODE] block or item ([ICODE]#tag[/ICODE] works), [ICODE]w:[/ICODE] world, [ICODE]at:[/ICODE] position; [ICODE]#count[/ICODE] and [ICODE]#verbose[/ICODE] flags.
[*]Rollback, restore and undo for blocks and for container items. A broken chest comes back with its contents. Big changes wait for [ICODE]/jo confirm[/ICODE], [ICODE]#preview[/ICODE] shows ghost blocks only to you.
[*]Changes are applied chunk by chunk within a time budget per tick; the rollback itself is not logged again.
[*]SQLite out of the box, MariaDB/MySQL for shared databases. Writes are batched on one background thread and never block the server.
[*]English and Czech messages, a config that updates itself (admin values and comments are kept), optional update check, bStats with an off switch.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Commands & Permissions[/B][/SIZE]
Root command /justlogs, aliases /jo and /jlogs. Everything is op by default.
[LIST]
[*][B]/justlogs inspect[/B] - Inspector mode: left click a block for its history, right click the spot next to it; right click a container for its item history (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.inspect[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs lookup <parameters>[/B] - Search the log (aliases [ICODE]/jo[/ICODE], [ICODE]/jlogs[/ICODE]; [ICODE]/jo l[/ICODE]) (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.lookup[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs near [radius][/B] - Changes around you in the last 3 days (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.lookup[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs page <n>[/B] - Another page of the last result (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.lookup[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs rollback <parameters>[/B] - Put blocks (or container items with [ICODE]a:container[/ICODE]) back; needs [ICODE]t:[/ICODE] and [ICODE]u:[/ICODE] or [ICODE]r:[/ICODE]; [ICODE]#preview[/ICODE] shows it to you first (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.rollback[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs restore <parameters>[/B] - Apply rolled-back changes again (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.restore[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs undo[/B] - Reverse your last rollback or restore (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.rollback[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs confirm / cancel[/B] - Run or drop a rollback that is waiting for confirmation (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.rollback or .restore[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs tp <world> <x> <y> <z>[/B] - Target of the clickable coordinates in results (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.teleport[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs status[/B] - Queue, writer, database and rollback state (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.status[/ICODE])
[*][B]/justlogs reload[/B] - Reload config.yml and messages (permission: [ICODE]justlogs.reload[/ICODE])
[*][ICODE]justlogs.lookup.container[/ICODE] - Look up container contents
[*][ICODE]justlogs.lookup.global[/ICODE] - Search the whole server, radius above the limit
[*][ICODE]justlogs.rollback.global[/ICODE] - Roll back without a radius
[*][ICODE]justlogs.helper[/ICODE] - Package: look things up, never change anything
[*][ICODE]justlogs.admin[/ICODE] - Everything
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Configuration[/B][/SIZE]
Everything is in [ICODE]plugins/JustLogs/config.yml[/ICODE] and commented: [ICODE]logging.*[/ICODE] switches for every logger, [ICODE]queue.*[/ICODE], [ICODE]lookup.*[/ICODE], [ICODE]rollback.*[/ICODE] (confirmation limit, time budget per tick, preview size) and [ICODE]storage.*[/ICODE]. New options are added to your existing file on update.

[SIZE=5][B]Known limits[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Rolling back a container only restores its item contents; other tile entities (signs, banners, heads, spawners) are not restored yet.
[*]WorldEdit logging is planned.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Compatibility[/B][/SIZE]
Paper / Purpur, Minecraft 26.3. Block and container rollback, restore and undo were tested across independent regions on Folia 26.3. The first start needs internet access: database drivers are downloaded once from Maven Central.
